> The most beautiful deeply-nested conditional I've ever seen

## Why

- The most agency I've seen in a visual novel
- Great writing brilliantly voiced
- Art style fits the vibe so well
- Short enough that you can finish it to get the idea, long enough that you can finish it a bunch more once you fall in love with it

## Why Not

- Can be gruesome and confronting at times

## Impressions

This game will hold up a mirror to you, sometimes literally. Given imperfect information, what will you do in response to increasingly strange situations? Are you a hero, a die-hard contrarian or (like the subreddit) just far too horny? There are so many different paths to take that just imagining what the code looks like gives me a headache, and they're all brilliantly written with top-notch voice acting. The Smitten was my favourite; and absolutely scenery-chewing performance, but every VA was great. You can roll credits in a few hours (or a few minutes for the Good Ending) but I challenge you to not immediately start again and continue exploring the labyrinthian story the devs have constructed here. I know I will, however painful certain paths may be after taking others first.
