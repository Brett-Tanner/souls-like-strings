> Nostalgia tricked me into liking a puzzle game

## Why

- Nails the early internet vibes
- Great worldbuilding, even outside the main plot
- Interesting plot delivered through interesting means
- HAP is exactly what I wanted at the end, to see what I missed

## Why Not

- You like a clear path forward at all times

## Impressions

I would've enjoyed this game if it was just the browser populated with all its cool old sites. Wandering around through what felt like a microcosm of the early internet, complete with all its jank and confusion, was quite an experience. The devs managed some great worldbuilding delivered entirely through people's personal pages, complete with fledgling businesses, drama and corporations trying to cash in on the next big thing. It's great fun to just methodically explore the various spaces, taking your time to immerse yourself in the vibes.

If that's not enough for you there is gameplay and an overarching plot though. You're essentially a Reddit mod, complete with petty tyrant mentality that seems to induce. After a few rounds of playing internet janitor (sometimes literally) you start to realise the startup behind Hypnospace is doing startup things, bringing you into conflict with the 'wunderkind' programmer behind it all. Interestingly the story can play out very differently (from your perspective) depending on how thoroughly you've explored the secrets of Hypnospace. If, like me, you did some digging outside your regular duties you might have some idea of what's up when it all goes down, but it's entirely possible to know exactly what's going on or have no idea depending on the effort you put in to finding secrets.

Definitely give Hypnospace a go if it appeals to you, you won't be disappointed.
