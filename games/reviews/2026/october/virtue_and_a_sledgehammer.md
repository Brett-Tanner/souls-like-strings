> Percussive therapy

At first they attack you, so you attack them.

This is a game after all, and you've been furnished with a satisfyingly weighty sledgehammer.

Some won't limit themselves to physical attacks, explaining why you might want to take a sledgehammer to your hometown.

But even those comments are distinctly human, and not every 'enemy' is out to get you.

At some point you'll find yourself wondering if you're really the hero of this story, before the pause menu reassures you "everyone you know is dead".

Eventually, unless you're a monster, you'll find yourself facing something you don't want to smash.

When all you have is a sledgehammer, everything looks like a target for demolition.
