- Skipped last week because of Silver Week + 2 days annual leave
- Git tracks file permissions
- Apple makes it way too easy to find 'developer docs' that read like marketing materials
  - And way too hard to find the actual docs even from there
  - They also steer you hard toward their preferred way of doing things by kinda implying other ways aren't possible
  - i.e. why would I need an app in the App Store to use OIDC against someone's Apple account???
  - but the docs heavily imply this is the case
- If there's an accessor for an attribute but no writer, you can still modify the contents of the value directly if it's a Hash/Array
- You can technically give an IAM account permission to work with a resource in another AWS account
  - But you need that permission in both the resource's policy and the IAM user's policy
- MDN's docs are surprisingly lacking in one area: DATA frames
  - They're mentioned as making the `Content-Length` header redundant on [its page](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTTP/Reference/Headers/Content-Length)
  - But there's no link explaining what they are
  - Though in fairness to MDN, documentation about what exactly they are and how they can be used in place of `Content-Length` is lacking in general based on my googling
