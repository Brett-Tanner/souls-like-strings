- Setting your `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` headers in Rails is (probably) better DX than doing it in nginx
  - But if you then enforce file-size limits in nginx you have to set the header in the same block, otherwise your frontend application won't see the `413` because of a CORS error
  - Making this worse, the `413` shows up perfectly fine in the Network tab of devtools
- One of the many issues with coworkers vibe coding everything is that they of course never run anything locally themselves
  - So their vibe coded changes often break local setups that people less prone to AI psychosis rely on to test their changes
- For the 10 millionth time, don't set `display: flex` on the `dialog` itself
  - Or if you do, at least scope it to `dialog[open]`
  - Also, it's `dialog.showModal`. Not `show`, or `open`.
- Guest Service CI does not check required secrets exist on PRs
