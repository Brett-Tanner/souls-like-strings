[Like this but Funnier](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Like-This-But-Funnier/Hallie-Cantor/9781668088586) by Hallie Cantor.

> Compound interest works on mistakes too

Quite a fun read, I never expected to relate so much to someone who stalks their husband's therapy patient and uses their murder dreams to pitch a shitty TV comedy but here we are. I can definitely believe Hollywood works as portrayed and even if it doesn't the main thing is it's an entertaining backdrop to Caroline eternally spiralling from the small mistake of accidentally glimpsing her husband's patient notes. Probably won't leave a mark on me like [Infinite Jest](/books/2025/infinite_jest.md) for example, but a very fun read nonetheless.
