[The Song of Achilles](https://madelinemiller.com/the-song-of-achilles/) by Madeline Miller.

> More like Achilles 'the Heel'

The second book by Madeline Miller means my second time spending a whole book recreating that meme of Leo DiCaprio pointing at the TV as a I recognise a bunch of characters from [Hades](/games/reviews/2025/hades_2.md). Unlike Hades, Miller doesn't gloss over the realities of the time, leading to some absolutely brutal moments and lending the plot a sense of stakes which just can't be present in a roguelike. Never thought I'd dislike my guy Odysseus after Hades 2 but this book makes it clear it's not fun being on the other side of his scheming.

To stop comparing it to a video game for a second, the book is well written and I definitely got attached to the characters. Miller has a real talent for bringing these Greek tragedies to life, as evidenced by Circe, and at least for my sake I hope she continues to do so.

Just checked and it turns out she has, will add Galatea and Mestra to my steadily shrinking backlog.
