[All the Living and the Dead](https://www.hayleycampbell.com/all-the-living-and-the-dead) by Haley Campbell.

> You might not enjoy reading it, but you'll be glad you did

Most people spend their lives trying to avoid thinking about death; Haley Campbell may have tried a little too hard to do the opposite. Many chapters in this book lead me to think 'huh, I guess it makes sense that exists' about jobs I never would have considered without reading it. Many chapters also made me more than a little squeamish, or sad, or uncomfortable. Despite this I'm glad I read it, and I think most people would benefit from opening their eyes about the realities of life's only guarantee by doing so.

Also, the author has a real knack for writing. Definitely has that innate feeling of 'quality' you get from good fiction authors which was a pleasant surprise for non-fiction.
