[James](https://www.panmacmillan.com/authors/percival-everett/james/9781035031269) by Percival Everett.

> You have to wonder what Mark Twain would think

The Wife not only picked a second consecutive Percival Everett book, but one telling the story of the missing 3rd character in Mark Twain's Adventures of Tom and Huck series (which I also read fairly recently). If you've read a Percival Everett book before you can probably guess our protagonist is Jim, and you can probably also guess he'll be quite different to Twain's portrayal.

I quite enjoyed this one; though it's heavy as expected for a story told from the perspective of a black man in this period of history there's some degree of satisfying comeuppance for the villains and seeing a familiar story from such an unfamiliar perspective was interesting. Again, there are definitely some heavy/confronting scenes here, but if you can get through them there's also another side to a well known story that's definitely worth hearing.
