[The Trees](https://www.panmacmillan.com/authors/percival-everett/the-trees/9781035036615) by Percival Everett.

> It took an embarassingly long time to figure out what 'The Trees' were

The story was all over the place but this was an interesting read regardless; despite the characters being kinda charicatures of the roles they played it was still enjoyable to read all their interactions. The central mystery was interesting until I realised it was very much not a mystery story, and the overall theme of 'horrible racists getting what they deserve' was satisfying.
