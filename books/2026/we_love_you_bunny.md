[We Love You, Bunny](https://monaawadauthor.com/book) by Mona Awad.

> Sometimes seeing things from the villain's perspective(s) makes them even worse

A prequel of sorts to [Bunny](/books/2026/bunny.md); though technically set after it. The first chapter reveals Bunny was an actual book published in-universe and the antagonists of said story took exception to their portrayal. Being normal, well adjusted people they express this by kidnapping Samantha, tying her up and recounting the chain of events which lead to them converting exploded bunnies into hot but empty men while holding an axe to her throat. We also learn of the existence of Aerius, the first of these unfortunate bunnies who acts as a POV character for large chunks of the book.

Overall I liked the book; it's definitely different from Bunny since there are now 5 POV characters rather than the intense focus on Samantha, and it felt much longer, but the structure was definitely an interesting approach to a prequel/sequel without being just more of the same. The Bunnies taking turns relating their tragic backstories dragged a bit in the beginning, but once Aerius' perspective is introduced the action certainly picks up. Overall a worthwhile followup that you should read if you enjoyed the first; even if it didn't hit quite the same for me.
